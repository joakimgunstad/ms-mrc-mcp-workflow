# D365 & Power Platform Roadmap – Week 45 2026

_Baseline / current landscape · generated 2026-11-05 · source: Microsoft AI at Work Roadmap via MRC MCP_


## TL;DR
- **Baseline issue.** This is the first run, so there's no week-over-week diff yet. It shows the current landscape for D365 CRM and Power Platform (193 roadmap items, Contact Center excluded). From next week on, only changes are shown.
- **Act now (admin enable):** the model-driven app *header and navigation refresh* is rolling out to GA ([576781](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=576781)). Plan testing and user comms. The Field Service mobile subgrid upgrade ([570641](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=570641)) also needs enabling.
- **Customer Service reporting is moving** to the new shared analytics (lakehouse) foundation ([574112](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=574112), [574114](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=574114)). Review existing Power BI reports.
- **Customer Service autonomous email resolution has launched** ([578064](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=578064)). It handles emails without creating a case by default, a big change to the email-to-case pattern. Review governance before enabling it.
- **29 items are past their GA target but still 'In development'**, mostly Copilot Studio (cost visibility, human approval for tools, Dataverse/SQL knowledge sources). Expect these to land or be re-dated soon.
